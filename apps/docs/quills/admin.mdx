---
title: "Set up a customer portal"
description: "Enable feedback, publish customer content, and invite customers to a private portal."
---

The portal gives customers access to feedback and support without making them workspace members.
Each workspace has its own portal. A customer signs in with a Kaneo account.

## Enable the portal

Portals start disabled. Use the API with a workspace member who has `quills:manage_settings`.
API keys also need the corresponding scope. See [API authentication](/api-reference/authentication).
The teammate settings pages are planned separately; these instructions use the current API.

1. Read `GET /quills/{workspaceId}/settings/portal`.
2. Send `PUT /quills/{workspaceId}/settings/portal` with `{"enabled": true}`.
3. Open `/portal/{workspaceId}` on your web app's public origin.

The paths above are relative to the API base URL. For a bundled installation, that base ends in
`/api`. Set the client URL to the web app's public URL so mail links reach the portal using the [environment configuration](/core/installation/environment-variables).

To turn the portal off, send the same update with `{"enabled": false}`. Disabling access does not
delete the workspace's feedback or support history.

## Choose who can enter

Use `PUT /quills/{workspaceId}/settings/portal/access` to set `visibility` to `public` or `private`.
The same operation accepts `allowedDomains` and `allowedSegmentIds` for access policies.
A private portal asks visitors to sign in and checks their access before showing protected content.
Published status and help-center content have their own visibility rules; do not use the portal
setting as a substitute for reviewing what you publish.

Anonymous interaction is off unless you enable `allowAnonymous` in the portal settings.
Review this setting before sharing a public link.

## Invite a customer

Send `POST /quills/{workspaceId}/portal/invites` with the customer's `email` and, optionally,
a `message` of up to 500 characters. The server queues the invitation email after the change commits.
Your instance must have working mail delivery.

The recipient must sign in with an account whose verified email matches the invitation. Accepting
the invitation grants portal access only. It does not grant access to projects or workspace settings.

Use `GET /quills/{workspaceId}/portal/invites` to view invitations. For a pending invitation:

- `POST /quills/{workspaceId}/portal/invites/{inviteId}/resend` sends it again and extends its expiry.
- `POST /quills/{workspaceId}/portal/invites/{inviteId}/link` creates a link to share privately.
- `DELETE /quills/{workspaceId}/portal/invites/{inviteId}` cancels it and invalidates its links.

An accepted invitation cannot be canceled through this endpoint. To remove that customer's access,
block the customer. Treat invitation links as private and never paste them into public logs or tickets.

## Branding and supported URLs

The portal uses the public logo, supported theme colors, and header display name from workspace
branding. Check text contrast when choosing colors. The portal does not apply `customCss`.

Use URLs on the configured web app origin. Custom portal and help-center domains are not supported
by these pages yet. Portal interface text is currently English. Help-center and changelog pages
render in the browser; search engines that do not run JavaScript may not index their full content.

Before sharing the portal, follow [deployment checks](/quills/deployment) and test a customer account
that is not a workspace member.

## Review duplicate posts

Open **Feedback > Duplicates** in the workspace (`/dashboard/workspace/{workspaceId}/quills/duplicates`).
The queue shows pairs of posts that the assistant found similar, strongest match first.
You need `quills:read` to see the queue and `quills:update` to decide.

- **Merge** merges the duplicate into the post to keep. Votes on the duplicate then count on the kept post.
- **Dismiss** removes the pair from the pending queue. **Restore** puts a dismissed pair back.

The queue shows only pairs whose two posts are on boards that you can see.
The API is `GET /quills/{workspaceId}/merge-suggestions?status=pending` (or `dismissed`).

In the portal, a signed-in customer who types a title that strongly matches a post sees
**Vote for this instead** next to that post, if the customer can vote on its board.

## Read feedback insights

Open **Feedback > Insights** (`/dashboard/workspace/{workspaceId}/quills/insights`) and select a range
of 7, 30, 90 or 180 days. The page shows the most used tags on new posts, the sentiment of new posts,
new posts and votes for each week, and the posts with the most new votes.
It counts only the boards that you can see. Sentiment counts only the posts that the assistant analyzed.

The API is `GET /quills/{workspaceId}/feedback/insights`. `from` and `to` are UTC days, at most
180 days apart. The default is the last 30 days.

## Review the feedback autopilot

When a conversation closes or a ticket is resolved, the assistant reads the customer's messages and
finds feature requests and bug reports. Each one becomes a pending signal with a short excerpt,
a suggested title and, when the instance has an embedding model, the most similar post.
The assistant reads only what the customer wrote: never team notes or replies.
Without an AI provider, the autopilot does nothing.

Open **Feedback > Autopilot** (`/dashboard/workspace/{workspaceId}/quills/autopilot`).
You need `quills:read` to see the queue, `quills:create` to accept and `quills:update` to dismiss or restore.

- **Accept as new post** creates a post on a board that you choose, with the title that you can edit.
  The customer is the author and the first voter.
- **Add vote** adds the customer's vote to the matched post.
- **Dismiss** removes the signal from the pending queue. **Restore** puts a dismissed signal back.

The queue leaves out signals of deleted tickets and test customers. A matched post shows only when its
board is visible to you. The API is `GET /quills/{workspaceId}/feedback-signals?status=pending`
(or `accepted`, `dismissed`), with an optional `source` of `conversation` or `ticket`.
