---
title: "Use your customer portal"
description: "Share feedback, follow updates, and contact support."
---

Open the portal link supplied by the team. A public portal may let you browse without an account.
A private portal asks you to sign in and must grant you access before it shows protected content.

## Accept an invitation

Open the invitation email and sign in or create an account using the invited email address.
Verify your email if asked. You can complete email verification in a new tab.
Return to the invitation page and select the confirmation action to accept.

Opening a mail link does not accept an invitation or submit a rating. You must confirm the action.
If a link has expired, ask the team for a new invitation.
If you signed in with a different email address, sign out and use the invited address.

## Share and follow feedback

Choose a board to browse posts. Open a post to read its details and comments.
Sign in to submit feedback, vote, or comment. The portal may also permit anonymous interaction,
depending on the team's settings. Use the submission form to describe the problem and check for
similar posts before sending a new one.

Your own posts and comments have edit and delete actions when the team's policy permits them.
Some workspaces restrict changes after other customers have engaged with a post.
Use the load-more control to read additional results.

A team can ask you a short survey above the posts. Choose a score or an answer, add a comment
if you want to, and send it. You answer each survey once. Select **Not now** to hide the survey
until you close the browser.

## Read updates and get help

The changelog lists published updates. The status page shows service status and incidents.
Open the help center to browse categories, read articles, or search for an answer.
Some content requires access even when you can open the help-center home page.

Use Support to start or return to your conversations and tickets. Open a conversation to read
messages and reply. Keep the page visible to receive its periodic updates.

## Manage your account and mail

Open Profile and Preferences to review your customer profile and subscriptions.
An unsubscribe link in a message opens a confirmation page. Select Unsubscribe to confirm.
The action applies to the subscription identified by that email link.

A satisfaction email opens a rating page. Choose a rating and confirm it to submit your response.
If the page says the link is no longer available, ask the team for help.

Sign out when using a shared computer. If an action page is embedded in another site, open the
portal directly in a new tab: confirmation and form submissions are disabled inside frames.
